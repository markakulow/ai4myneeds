Review My Resume

For our first guided workflow, gather your current resume and use the steps below. You stay in control of every change.

1

Start with your real resume.
Use the version you actually send to employers. Don't “improve” facts before the review.

2

Decide what you want reviewed.
Clarity, accomplishments, leadership impact, formatting, length, or overall effectiveness.

3

Use AI as a reviewer, not a ghostwriter.
Ask it to identify opportunities and questions before rewriting anything.

4

Verify every recommendation.
Keep only changes that accurately reflect what you did and can comfortably discuss in an interview.

STARTER PROMPT

Try this with your resume

Act as a candid resume reviewer. Review the resume I provide for clarity, impact, relevance and readability. Do not invent or assume any experience, skills, metrics or accomplishments. First identify the strongest areas, then the areas that need improvement. Ask me questions when additional facts would help strengthen a bullet rather than creating those facts yourself. Explain your recommendations before rewriting anything.
