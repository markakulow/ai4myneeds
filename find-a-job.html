Find a Job

Make your job search more focused, not more exhausting.

Use AI to explore possibilities, search more intelligently and evaluate opportunities while keeping the decisions grounded in your real experience and priorities.

✓

AI can help you search and think. It can't decide fit for you. Use it to uncover options and questions, then verify current job and company information before acting on it.

How this works

Start with where you're stuck.

You don't need to overhaul your entire job search. Pick one problem below and use AI4MyNeeds to make that part easier.

Job search help

What would you like help with?

Each guide gives you a practical way to use AI without pretending AI is the job board, recruiter or hiring manager.

🎯

Figure out what roles fit me

Discover job titles and career directions that connect to your actual experience, strengths and preferences.

Try this: Give your AI assistant a summary of your work history, strengths, work you enjoy, work you want less of, location/remote needs and compensation range. Ask it for role families and titles worth researching, including less-obvious options. Have it explain why each might fit and what gaps you should investigate rather than assuming you're qualified.

🔎

Search smarter

Turn your background into better titles, keywords and searches for job boards and company career sites.

Try this: Provide several target roles plus your experience. Ask AI for related titles, alternate terminology, industry keywords and Boolean search ideas. Use those searches on real job platforms, then refine them based on the postings you actually find.

🧩

Is this job actually a good fit?

Compare a posting with your real background and separate strong matches, transferable skills and genuine gaps.

Try this: Give AI the full job description and your resume. Ask for three sections: Strong Matches, Transferable/Underrepresented Experience and Genuine Gaps. Tell it not to invent qualifications or treat every preferred requirement as mandatory. Use the result to decide whether the opportunity deserves more research or an application.

🏢

Research a company

Know what to investigate before you apply, interview or decide whether an employer interests you.

Try this: Ask AI to create a research checklist covering what the company does, customers, leadership, recent news, financial/business signals, culture claims and questions or risks worth investigating. For anything current, verify it using the company's website and reliable recent sources rather than relying only on an AI model's memory.

📋

Organize my job search

Create a simple system for applications, networking, follow-ups and the work you want to do each week.

Try this: Tell AI how much time you realistically have each week and the activities you're currently doing. Ask it to help create a manageable weekly routine plus a tracker for company, role, link, date applied, contact, status, next action and follow-up date. Keep the system simple enough that you'll actually maintain it.

Useful job-search resources

AI works better alongside the right sources.

Use AI for discovery, comparison, preparation and organization. Use current job boards, employer career pages and professional networks to verify openings and make real connections.

🌐

Job platforms

Search multiple sources instead of assuming one platform has the whole market. Save useful searches and refine them as you learn which titles produce the right opportunities.

🤝

Your network

AI can help you prepare outreach, but the relationship is human. Look for former coworkers, industry contacts, recruiters and people connected to organizations you're genuinely interested in.

🏠

Company career pages

When an opportunity matters, verify the opening and details at the employer itself. It can also reveal related roles that never surfaced in your original search.
