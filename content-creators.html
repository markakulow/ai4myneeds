🎬 CONTENT CREATORS

Make more room for creativity. Spend less time staring at a blank page.

Whether you create videos, posts, podcasts, newsletters or something entirely your own, AI can help you plan, explore ideas and streamline the work around creating.

What are you trying to create?

Pick a starting point and try one small experiment. You don't need a full production workflow.

💡

Find fresh content ideas

Ask AI for angles based on your audience, interests and what you want people to take away.

Try asking: “I create content about [topic] for [audience]. Suggest 10 useful ideas that answer real questions, not clickbait. Explain why each might matter.”

🎥

Plan a video

Turn a rough idea into an outline, opening hook, key points and a shot list. Keep your own voice.

Try asking: “Help me outline a 60-second video about [topic]. Ask me about my audience and message first.”

✍️

Polish a post or script

Improve clarity and structure without turning your writing into generic AI-sounding copy.

Try asking: “Edit this draft for clarity and flow. Preserve my tone and don't invent facts or examples.”

📅

Organize a content schedule

Batch ideas, plan realistic production time and reuse good work across formats.

Try asking: “Help me build a manageable two-week content plan for [platform] with [hours] available each week.”

Keep the creator in the driver's seat

Check facts, respect copyright and permissions, and avoid sharing private audience or client information. AI can assist with the process; your judgment, taste and experience are what make the content yours.
